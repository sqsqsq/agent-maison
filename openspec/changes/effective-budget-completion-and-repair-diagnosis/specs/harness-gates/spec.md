## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Weakening-flag reports are judged by their conclusion, not by trace-faithful status words

Under a weakening flag (`--skip-assert-expected`), the test-report SHALL keep projecting the Hylyre trace four-state table verbatim (报告/报告 "通过" included), with the flag disclosed. The report SHALL count from the structured trace, separately, explicit assertions (`role=assertion`, `outcome.status=passed`) and natural-language expectations skipped by the flag (`role=assertion`, skipped), and the weakening disclosure SHALL qualify only the skipped part; when the trace has no structured assertion steps the previous whole-report weakening wording SHALL remain. While the flag is present the report SHALL always record one gap stating the skipped count, the passed explicit assertion count and that the conclusion is not signed 达标, so the generated report and the disclosure check agree even when nothing was skipped. `pass_rate_calculated` SHALL NOT fail `reportedPass > 0` per se; instead it SHALL fail when the weakening flag is present AND the report conclusion (parsed by the existing conclusion parser) declares 达标 — action-chain success SHALL NOT wash into full acceptance. Missing disclosure, overall-rate overclaim, trace/report mismatches, and the fresh negative-summary contradiction SHALL remain FAIL; the trace ceiling and P0 full-denominator constraints stay.

Enforcement: `harness/scripts/check-testing.ts`, `harness/scripts/utils/testing-trace-gates.ts`, `profiles/hmos-app/harness/test-report-writer.ts`

#### Scenario: a faithful trace projection with a negative conclusion passes the report gate

- **WHEN** the command carries `--skip-assert-expected`, the table projects the trace's 通过 verbatim with disclosure, and the conclusion is 不达标
- **THEN** `pass_rate_calculated` SHALL PASS (same verdict through the `quality-axes.ts` report-validity consumption); changing the conclusion to 达标 SHALL FAIL directly without relying on a fresh negative summary

#### Scenario: explicit assertions are not described as action-chain only

- **WHEN** a trace holds 75 passed explicit assertions and 6 natural-language expectations skipped by the flag
- **THEN** the report SHALL state both counts separately, qualify only the 6 skipped expectations as unverified, and still not sign 达标

#### Scenario: a flag with zero skips stays consistent with the gate

- **WHEN** the flag is present, nothing was skipped and there is no other gap
- **THEN** the generated report SHALL conclude 有条件达标 with the flag gap as its only gap, and the disclosure check SHALL return no finding

### Requirement: The testing report is generated by the harness and concludes per axis

`test-report.md` SHALL be generated by the harness from the authoritative run's trace, timing, build/install/run metadata, the gaps ledger, `visual-diff.json`, `visual-debt.json`, geometry measurement when present, the current quality axes and the stability data when present. Every section SHALL be deterministic and readable back by the existing report parsers. The conclusion SHALL be derived per axis — functional PASS/FAIL, interaction stability PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN, visual geometry PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN, visual content CHECKED/UNKNOWN, visual style CHECKED/UNKNOWN, known gaps — with an overall `COMPLETE` / `COMPLETE_WITH_GAPS` / `FAILED`. Incomplete visual evidence SHALL NOT block functional completion, and a report SHALL NOT claim UX conformance from the trace alone.

Visual facts SHALL be read from the authorization-filtered effective view (`effectiveScreens` with the repair authority scope), not raw verdicts. Without a measurement file, geometry SHALL be PASS when every relevant screen is pass or an effective `minor` warn, FAIL when any is fail, otherwise UNKNOWN. Content and style SHALL be CHECKED only when every relevant screen has a finalized verdict with `evaluated_screenshot_hash`, is not stale by screenshot hash and current build fingerprint (`isStaleVisualDiffVerdict`), and the canonical receipt `device-testing/reports/critic-receipt.json` passes the gate's own shape validation and its `image_inputs[].hash` covers each current screenshot hash; file existence alone SHALL NOT count. Minor residual screens SHALL be listed as 披露 in the defect table and SHALL NOT be counted as 待修复. The overall SHALL be `FAILED` when any visual check produced by the testing gate's visual block in this round (plus capture-stage `visual_diff*` results), judged by `isBlockingCheck`, is blocking, naming that check in the gaps; soft-tier visual debt without a blocking gate result SHALL stay `COMPLETE_WITH_GAPS`. The writer SHALL NOT derive a second visual conclusion of its own.

Agent observations SHALL live in `testing/notes.md`, which SHALL NOT participate in gates, closure, subject or freshness. `--report-reconcile-only` SHALL regenerate the machine report. The review report's statistics table SHALL be recomputed by the checker, and citation/count lints SHALL be WARN only.

Enforcement: `profiles/hmos-app/harness/test-report-writer.ts`, `profiles/hmos-app/harness/visual-diff-check.ts`, `harness/scripts/utils/testing-trace-gates.ts`, `harness/scripts/check-review.ts`, `harness/scripts/check-testing.ts`

#### Scenario: Functional pass with visual failure is not COMPLETE

- **WHEN** all 20 cases pass but `visual-diff.json` holds a FAIL screen
- **THEN** the generated conclusion SHALL show functional PASS, visual geometry FAIL and an overall that is not `COMPLETE`

#### Scenario: Bound provider review is reported as checked

- **WHEN** every relevant screen has a current finalized verdict and the canonical receipt covers each current screenshot hash
- **THEN** content and style SHALL be CHECKED; changing one screenshot byte or the installed HAP SHALL turn them UNKNOWN

#### Scenario: A blocking visual gate result fails the report

- **WHEN** the hard-tier testing gate returns a blocking `visual_diff` or `runtime_mount_conformance` result in this round
- **THEN** the report written through the production regeneration path SHALL conclude 不达标 and name the blocking check, while a soft-tier WARN with open visual debt SHALL conclude 有条件达标

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Ad-hoc repair diagnosis modes stay behind the entry gate and default to `_adhoc`

The ad-hoc device CLI SHALL offer `--install-current-build` and `--measure-screen <screen_id> --feature <f>` (missing `--feature` is a usage error), usable alone or with `--dump-ui-only` / `--observe-ui` / execution. Both SHALL run only after the existing entry device gate has passed and before any other device step that depends on them; a failed gate SHALL stop with the existing reason and no build or device command. `--install-current-build` SHALL only orchestrate the existing `runDeviceTestAppBuild` and `installDeviceTestApp` with feature `_adhoc` and phase `testing`, without passing product or build mode (the providers resolve them with the same resolver as testing; an unresolved product SHALL stop with the existing guidance), forcing a real install in-process and restoring the environment afterwards; the full HAP digest SHALL be returned without adding a meta field. `--measure-screen` SHALL capture screenshot and layout through the existing capture builders into `_adhoc/testing/reports/`, compute with the pure `measureScreen`, read the feature's ui-spec read-only, and MUST NOT call `runVisualMeasure`, `fillVisualDiffDefectNotes` or `captureVisualDiff`. The default `--dump-ui-only` output SHALL be `doc/features/_adhoc/testing/reports/dump-ui-<timestamp>.json` (no longer the shared app-snapshot cache), and the observe path SHALL pass the same output option. These outputs SHALL NOT be formal evidence: formal screenshots, `visual-diff.json`, the critic receipt, testing reports and the phase evidence manifest SHALL be unchanged, and testing SHALL build, install and judge by its own rules.

Enforcement: `harness/scripts/adhoc-device-test.ts`, `harness/scripts/utils/adhoc-repair-diagnosis.ts`, `harness/scripts/utils/adhoc-dump-ui.ts`

#### Scenario: Installing the current build leaves formal evidence untouched

- **WHEN** coding runs `--install-current-build` twice and then measures a screen
- **THEN** both installs SHALL be real, `_adhoc/testing/reports/` SHALL hold the build result, install meta and measurement, every formal testing / device-testing evidence file SHALL be byte-identical, and the next testing install SHALL reinstall because the HAP changed

#### Scenario: A device that is not ready stops at the gate

- **WHEN** `--install-current-build` runs with no device policy configured
- **THEN** the CLI SHALL stop with `device_policy_unset`, exit non-zero without a stack trace, and SHALL NOT build
